HOW IT ANSWERS

Smart Priority Chain

Every question is checked step by step until the best answer is found.

1

Custom Q&A

Matches your admin-defined questions by keywords.

Tap again to collapse. You define exact question/answer pairs in the admin panel — these are always checked first for instant, precise replies.
→
2

WooCommerce

Price, stock, search, categories, featured & sale items.

Reads your live product catalog directly — prices and stock are always accurate, never hardcoded.
→
3

Store Info

Contact, address & info from your pages.

Pulls contact details, hours, and policies straight from your existing site pages.
→
4

AI Answer

Open-ended questions, grounded in store data.

For anything not covered above, AI generates a helpful answer — grounded only in your actual store data.
→
5

Fallback

Polite decline for off-topic questions.

If nothing matches, the assistant politely explains it can't help with that — no made-up answers.
